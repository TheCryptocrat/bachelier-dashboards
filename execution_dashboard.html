How to read this
Slippage = filled price − requested price (the order's price_open), signed so that positive is worse for us. $ values use a per-symbol value-per-price-unit fitted from real closed round trips on these accounts.
Market vs Stop/Limit is split deliberately. Stop orders gap through their level in fast markets, so their slippage measures market conditions, not the broker. Only the Market rows say much about execution quality.
There is no spread column, because the pipeline never records bid/ask. A market order's slippage already absorbs the spread — that is why market fills skew adverse instead of scattering around zero — but spread cannot be separated out from what is captured today. Doing so would need EA-side spread logging or a tick-data join.
